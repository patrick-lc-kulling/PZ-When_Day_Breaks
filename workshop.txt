description=Features the liquefying Sun from the SCP Essay Series: "When Day Breaks" — cover your skin and patch the holes in your clothes if you don't wish to liquefy to [i]"death"[/i]
description=[hr][/hr]
description=Sunlight detection logic is based of SCP-001 "When Day Breaks" by Ten Thousand Bees. This version features a plethora of features to aid realism and playability as well as tweakable sandbox values to customize the experience to your own liking.
description=
description=The Sun will slowly "liquefy" — damage and apply burns — to exposed body parts, the rate of damage depends on the amount of and type of bodyparts, where an exposed head takes 3x the damage of others by default.
description=
description=[h2]Features:[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]A sun that will damage you during the day aswell as during moon phases near full moon and during the full moon: outside, aswell as in buildings if it can shine on you through uncovered windows.
description=
description=[*]Light Intensity scaled damage.
description=
description=[*]Tweakable sandbox settings.
description=
description=[*]Hole Detection logic, meaning: you will still be harmed by the sun if your full body cover gets holes down to skin level through scratches or lacerations.
description=
description=[*]Every head covering has its own protection value: a sealed helmet keeps the light out entirely, while masks, bandanas and respirators only soften it. [url=https://imgur.com/a/pz-mod-when-day-breaks-9ZqOQez]Headcovering Protection Value Table[/url]
description=
description=[*]Holding an open umbrella in either hand shields you, reducing the damage the light does by 30%.
description=
description=[*]A Feature that wakes you up automatically if you accidentaly leave a curtain open and start taking sun damage while sleeping.
description=
description=[*]All natural light from the Sun having a red tinge, while other light sources keep their colour.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Tips:[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Start at 12AM through the Sandbox settings so you're not immediately turned into a puddle.
description=
description=[*]If you can't find gloves and fully fitting helmets early on, then collect sewing materials, duct tape and trash bags or tarp to make yourself cheap handwraps and bag headsacks to partially protect yourself from the sun.
description=
description=[i]be aware though, tripping or getting attacked with such a flimsy covering might throw the bag off your head and expose you to the light.[/i]
description=
description=[*]Incase you want to spawn during daytime, Spawn with the clothes that cover the most amount of skin, so that you have more time to hide away from the light.
description=
description=[*]You can see the current moon phase through the Search GUI while standing outside at night.
description=[/list]
description=
